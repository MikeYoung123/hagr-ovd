# Upstream notice

HAGR-OVD is a research fork of Ultralytics 8.4.41 and contains modifications for SigLIP2 text features, role-aware partial-label training, HAGR routing, temporal training, and BridgeVLA-OVD evaluation.

The repository also builds on ideas and interfaces from YOLO-World, SigLIP2, BridgeData V2, Segment Anything, Hugging Face Transformers/Datasets, OpenCV, and PyTorch. Their names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. See the paper and README for citations and upstream links.
